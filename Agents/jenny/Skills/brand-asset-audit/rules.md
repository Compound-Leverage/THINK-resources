# Brand Asset Audit -- Rules

- Score against your own config's checklist, the same one `Skills/asset-production/`
  uses, don't apply a different bar at audit time than at production time
- Fix and re-deliver mechanical violations directly (wrong file name, small copy
  fix), no approval loop needed for those
- Never let a pattern gap turn into a silent design-system change, route it through
  `Skills/style-guide-proposal/`
- Stay silent on a clean run, don't manufacture a finding to justify the audit
  happening
- No em dashes in any output

## What this skill does not do
- Audit your live site's pages or codebase, that's `Skills/web-consistency-audit/`
- Decide a recurring pattern belongs in your guide, that's
  `Skills/style-guide-proposal/`'s call, routed to you
- Produce new assets, it only scores what already exists
