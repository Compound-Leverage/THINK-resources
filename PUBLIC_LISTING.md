# Public listing copy (ChatGPT / Claude directory submission)

Reference copy for the OpenAI/Codex public plugin directory submission form.
This describes the **curated 3-team public offering only** -- not the full
9-plugin marketplace in this repo (that stays available via direct GitHub-URL
install for THINK School members, per `README.md`).

## Structure

**THINK School Practitioner Resources**

- **ChatGPT:** Capture Team + Proposal Team + Fulfillment
- **Claude:** Capture Team + Proposal Team + Fulfillment
- **THINK School on Skool:** the complete environment -- full team library,
  Digital Employees, this GitHub repository, training, customization, team-building
  methodology, updates, community.

Guiding principle: **public plugins help practitioners do the work. THINK School
teaches practitioners how to build, customize, and manage the workforce.** The
free tier is not intentionally limited -- Capture Team is genuinely good at
capture. The upsell is the system, capability development, resources, and
community around it, not a fuller version of the same tool.

Distribution funnel: search/discovery -> ChatGPT or Claude plugin -> practitioner
gets a real outcome -> recognizes THINK School -> deeper need -> THINK School on
Skool. GitHub is the technical distribution/source layer underneath all of it.

## Listing name

**THINK School: AI Teams**

## Listing description

Ready-to-use AI Teams for capture, proposals, and fulfillment. Specialized
Digital Employees work together across the lifecycle from identifying
opportunities through developing proposals and supporting delivery.

## The three teams (submission scope)

**Capture -> Proposal -> Fulfillment**

1. **Capture Team** -- find, research, qualify, and pursue the right opportunities.
2. **Proposal Team** -- turn qualified opportunities into compliant, competitive submissions.
3. **Fulfillment** -- support execution after the work is won.

Deliberately stopping at three for the first public release. Three teams tell a
complete lifecycle story (win the work, do the work); adding Content, Sales BD,
Sales Enablement, Lead Discovery, or Ops to the public submission now would
dilute that story before the marketplace has proven out discovery and usage.
Revisit expanding the public roster only after that signal exists.
