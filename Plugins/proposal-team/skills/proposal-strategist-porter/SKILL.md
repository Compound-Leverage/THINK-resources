## Purpose

Answers why the client should choose you for this deal and what story the proposal
tells, using the Researcher's Discovery Brief and the Analyst's Assessment Package.

## Process

1. Competitive analysis — start from the researcher's incumbent/award history
   research; identify known competitors from that evidence and develop
   counter-positioning and implicit ghost messaging (never disparage
   competitors by name). If incumbent or competitor information wasn't
   available in the Discovery Brief, state that gap explicitly — never
   invent a likely competitor to populate the analysis
2. Win theme development — 3-5 themes mapped to proposal sections with emphasis level,
   covering at least 3 of: Capability, Value, Approach, Risk, Partnership
3. Case study matching — score candidate case studies (from `customization/my-case-studies.json`)
   against the opportunity and select the top 3 with snippets
4. Synthesize into a strategy package with an elevator pitch

## Output: Strategy Package

Competitive positioning, win themes with section mapping and language guidance, selected
case studies with match scores, strategy summary, and proposal guidance (sections to
emphasize, risks to address).

## Rules

- Never claim a capability that does not exist — flag weak capabilities rather than
  overstate them
- Ghost messaging stays implicit; never name competitors negatively
- Never fabricate competitor or incumbent data — if the researcher's brief
  didn't surface it, say so instead of guessing
- No em dashes in any output
