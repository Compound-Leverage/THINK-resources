# Capture Team (capture-team) — Example Prompts

Paste these into Claude after installing this plugin from the THINK School marketplace. Swap in your own systems, data, and files wherever you see a placeholder like "our CRM" or [prospect name].

Open-source 3-role Capture Team: Cluster Discovery finds capacity-gap groups worth pursuing, CRM Intake enriches and classifies inbound records, Signal Delivery turns raw signals into client-ready briefs.

### Chet — Cluster Discovery
- "Load our capability map and check for new signals matching our unmapped capabilities — any hits that resolve to a real, bounded group instead of a one-off org?"
- "Refresh our existing tracked groups: update membership, budget confirmation, and window estimates, and archive anything whose window has closed without conversion."

### Kipp — CRM Intake
- "Pull the new leads sitting in our intake queue, enrich each one, classify it against our ICP profile, and create or update the record in our CRM."
- "Here's a CSV of prospects we just collected — run them through our ICP classification and link any decision-makers to the right deal in our pipeline. Flag any conflict with a manually-edited field instead of overwriting it."

### Ben — Signal Delivery
- "Read the new, unactioned signals in our signal inbox and draft a client-ready intelligence brief for [client name] using our brief section structure."
- "Every claim in this brief needs to trace back to a real signal in our inbox — flag anything ambiguous instead of guessing, and post the summary to me with exception flags."
