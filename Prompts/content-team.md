# Content Team (content-team) — Example Prompts

Paste these into Claude after installing this plugin from the THINK School marketplace. Swap in your own systems, data, and files wherever you see a placeholder like "our CRM" or [prospect name].

Open-source 9-role content team: Intel Scanner feeds every downstream skill with scored topic cards; the rest turn those signals into newsletters, blog posts, LinkedIn content, YouTube scripts, intelligence briefs, monthly themes, a weekly owner brief, and full courses.

### Rohit — Intel Scanner
- "Scan Reddit, industry news, YouTube, and Google's 'People Also Ask' for the strongest content signals in our space this week, plus our own platform performance data, and give me the top 5 scored topic cards."
- "Flag any signal you're not confident about instead of inflating its score."

### Ann — Newsletter Editor
- "Take this week's top-scoring topic card and draft a WARM edition of our newsletter for engaged subscribers and a COLD edition for new subscribers, translated through our methodology framework."
- "Post both newsletter drafts to our newsletter platform as drafts — don't send them live."

### Cal — Weekly Brief
- "Pull together everything the content and BD skills produced this week and give me one page: what happened, what's pending, and what needs my decision."
- "Overwrite last week's brief with this week's — don't append to it."

### Jay — Theme Proposer
- "Based on our strongest signal clusters and current offer this month, propose 3 genuinely distinct content themes for next month, each scored against our weighting."
- "Write those 3 theme proposals as Pending records in our theme proposals database for me to approve."

### Joanna — Blog Writer
- "Take this week's top signal and write two long-form blog posts from it — one in our practitioner ICP voice, one in our org-buyer ICP voice — so neither reads generic."
- "End each post with the CTA style we've configured for that specific ICP."

### Josh — Intelligence Brief
- "Filter this week's topic cards to only the ones meeting our proof-gate threshold, then draft a Leader edition and a Strategist edition of our intelligence brief."
- "Flag any org-buyer-relevant topic from this week's brief so our BD team can follow up on it."

### Justin — LinkedIn Content
- "Generate a full week of LinkedIn posts for our personal brand page, company page, and product page from this week's signal stack, matching each persona's configured posting cadence."
- "Combine this week's posts into one queue file, grouped by persona and posting day, so I can schedule them."

### Amy — YouTube Scripts
- "Here's our research file and this week's brief — write a 4-5 minute YouTube script with 2-3 title options, 2-3 hook variants, a signal walkthrough, and a CTA close."
- "Check that this script actually runs 4-5 minutes read aloud before you finish."

### Sal — Course Builder
- "Here's my course brief for [topic] — build the complete course: outline, start-here module, lessons, exercises, capstone, and index, ready to paste into [our course platform]."
- "Run your final consistency pass across all the modules and flag anything missing from my brief instead of inventing content to fill the gap."
