# Fulfillment (fulfillment-open) — Example Prompts

Paste these into Claude after installing this plugin from the THINK School marketplace. Swap in your own systems, data, and files wherever you see a placeholder like "our CRM" or [prospect name].

Open-source 2-role fulfillment team: Signal Delivery turns raw signals into client-ready briefs, Playbook Builder produces onboarding/success/expansion playbooks for active clients.

### Ben — Signal Delivery
- "Read the new, unactioned signals in our signal inbox and draft a client-ready intelligence brief for [client name] using our brief section structure."
- "Every claim in this brief needs to trace back to a real signal in our inbox — flag anything ambiguous instead of guessing, and post the summary to me with exception flags."

### Lincoln — Playbook Builder
- "Using our onboarding playbook framework and [client]'s record in our CRM, build a customized onboarding playbook for them."
- "Build an expansion playbook for [client] based on their current engagement data. If a detail isn't in their record, mark it as a gap rather than inventing it."
