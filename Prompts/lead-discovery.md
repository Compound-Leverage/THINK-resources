# Lead Discovery (lead-discovery) — Example Prompts

Paste these into Claude after installing this plugin from the THINK School marketplace. Swap in your own systems, data, and files wherever you see a placeholder like "our CRM" or [prospect name].

Standalone skill that automates lead qualification for government contractors and economic development organizations, scoring opportunities across 5 dimensions (fit, win probability, economic value, timeline, strategic leverage) and surfacing decision-ready prospects.

### Lead Discovery
- "Score this RFP against our 5-dimension model and tell me whether to pursue, delegate outreach, or monitor: [paste opportunity details]."
- "Here are 10 opportunities we pulled from SAM.gov and our state procurement portal this week — qualify each one, drop anything scoring under 12 or hitting a disqualifier, and give me the ranked shortlist."
- "Evaluate this grant opportunity against our current capabilities and give me the full scoring breakdown plus a recommended next step."
