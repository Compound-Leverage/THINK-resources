# Proposal Generator (proposal-generator) — Example Prompts

Paste these into Claude after installing this plugin from the THINK School marketplace. Swap in your own systems, data, and files wherever you see a placeholder like "our CRM" or [prospect name].

Single all-in-one skill that runs the same 7-phase proposal methodology (intake, research, assessment, strategy, draft, QA, output) end to end for one high-value deal at a time.

### Proposal Generator
- "run proposal [Client Name] — here's their website, industry, due date, and how this opportunity came to us."
- "We're at the Research phase for [client] — here's what we know so far. Give me the Discovery Brief and your Go/No-Go recommendation."
- "Draft the full proposal for [client] using the commercial-style template, pulling from our filled-in pricing model, case studies, and brand guidelines."
