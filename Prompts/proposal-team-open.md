# Proposal Team (proposal-team-open) — Example Prompts

Paste these into Claude after installing this plugin from the THINK School marketplace. Swap in your own systems, data, and files wherever you see a placeholder like "our CRM" or [prospect name].

Open-source 7-role proposal team — orchestrator, researcher, analyst, strategist, writer, QA, and grant/funder pitch development — that turns a qualified opportunity into a submission-ready proposal with human approval gates between phases.

### Maya — Proposal Engine Lead
- "Run the full proposal pipeline for [prospect name] end to end — intake, research, assessment, strategy, draft, and QA — and stop for my approval at each gate."
- "Give me a status check on where our [prospect name] proposal is in the 7-phase workflow."

### Chase — Proposal Researcher
- "Research [prospect company] — client background, industry trends, compliance/regulatory issues, technology landscape, and key stakeholders — and give me a Discovery Brief with a Go/No-Go recommendation."
- "Flag any red flags like litigation, layoffs, or financial distress you find on [prospect company], and list what you couldn't find instead of guessing."

### Priya — Proposal Analyst
- "Here's the Discovery Brief for [prospect] — classify the deal type, map their requirements against our capabilities, and flag any gaps."
- "Using our pricing model and ROI defaults, build a conservative ROI model for [prospect] with break-even point and Year 1 ROI, using their own numbers where we have them."

### Porter — Proposal Strategist
- "Using the Discovery Brief and Assessment Package for [prospect], develop our competitive positioning, 3-5 win themes mapped to proposal sections, and pick our top 3 matching case studies."
- "Draft a 2-3 sentence elevator pitch for this deal based on the win themes you just built."

### Quinn — Proposal Writer
- "Draft the full commercial-style proposal for [prospect] from our research, assessment, and strategy packages. Mark any gap as a placeholder instead of assuming an answer."
- "Write the executive summary for this proposal last, problem-first, under 2 pages, using our brand voice and terminology rules."

### Diego — Proposal QA
- "Run a standard QA pass on this proposal draft for [prospect] — requirements coverage, number consistency, compliance, and methodology — and give me a PASS/WARNING/FAIL verdict with exact section citations."
- "Do a quick consistency-only check on this draft before I send it — do the investment and timeline figures match across every section?"

### Blair — Pitch Development
- "Convert this opportunity from our pipeline into a customized proposal for [funder category] — use our bid-sizing rules and our real case studies, not estimated ones."
- "What's the decision timeline and next contact date I should state in this funder proposal?"
