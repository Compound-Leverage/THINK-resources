# Standalone Skills — Example Prompts

Paste these into Claude after installing this plugin from the THINK School marketplace. Swap in your own systems, data, and files wherever you see a placeholder like "our CRM" or [prospect name].

### think-foundations
Guided 7-module course companion that walks a user conversationally through the THINK Foundations curriculum, building toward their first Digital Employee.
- "Start Module 1 — run the Thinking Crisis assessment for me."
- "Run Module 3, the DE Definition Card, using the bottleneck I identified in Module 1."
- "Walk me through Module 5, the THINK Framework Worksheet, for my biggest manual bottleneck."

### mandate-mapping (Mandate Search Agent)
Configurable funding-opportunity monitor that scores grants and contracts against your own business profile, geography, and industry, focused on catching pre-RFP signals before opportunities go competitive.
- "Help me set up my Mandate Search Agent configuration for my business in [state/region] and [industry]."
- "Search for funding opportunities in my target regions and industries and score each one against my configured priorities."
- "Generate my weekly digest of opportunities, tagged white-paper-ready, proposal-ready, nurture-needed, or monitor-only."

### Signal Finder Toolkit
Interactive 4-phase lead-magnet session that identifies a user's market blindspot, maps active capital events in their sector, captures their playbook foundation, and recommends one matched Digital Employee.
- "Run the Signal Finder Toolkit for my sector: [industry]."
- "Find my opportunity — I want to know where funding creates a window for my business and what Digital Employee I need to capture it."

### community-grant-radar
Three-phase grant, contract, and capital-event radar for any sector and geography — capital event signals, active grants, and active contract solicitations, each ending in a specific first move.
- "/radar [our sector] [our state] — run the full three-phase scan for us."
- "/score [paste a specific grant or contract URL] — score this opportunity against our profile."
- "/grants [our sector] [our state] — just show me active grants we could apply to right now."
