# Support — THINK School

- **Community & questions:** post in the
  [THINK School community](https://www.skool.com/thinkschool/about) (Wins, Q&A).
- **Partnerships / institutional licensing:**
  [compoundleverage.com/contact-sales](https://www.compoundleverage.com/contact-sales/)
  or [partners@compoundleverage.com](mailto:partners@compoundleverage.com).
- **Bugs or setup issues with a specific plugin:** check that plugin's
  `customization/` config is filled in and its `.mcp.json` connectors are
  authorized in your session first — most install issues trace back to one of
  those two. If that doesn't resolve it, post in the community with the plugin
  name and what you're seeing.
