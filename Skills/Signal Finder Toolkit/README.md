# Signal Finder Toolkit

A guided session that finds active grants, contracts, and pre-RFP opportunities in your sector, scores each one, and matches you to the right Digital Employee for your highest-leverage gap.

Takes about 10 minutes. Free to run.

---

## Setup Instructions

### Claude (Recommended)

1. Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and create a new Project
2. Upload `SKILL.md` and `scripts/score_event.py` as Knowledge files
3. Start a new conversation inside the Project
4. Type: `Run the Signal Finder Toolkit`

**Note:** Live opportunity search and scoring requires Claude Pro or higher. The free tier runs Phases 1, 3, and 4 but cannot search and score live funding opportunities in Phase 2.

---

### ChatGPT

1. Download `Signal Finder Toolkit.zip` from this folder
2. Open [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) and start a new conversation
3. Attach `SKILL.md` and `scripts/score_event.py` from the zip
4. Type: `Run the Signal Finder Toolkit` and press send

---

### Gemini

1. Download `Signal Finder Toolkit.zip` from this folder
2. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) and start a new conversation
3. Upload `SKILL.md` and `scripts/score_event.py` from the zip
4. Type: `Run the Signal Finder Toolkit` and press send

---

## What to Expect

**Phase 1 — Blindspot Scan**
5 questions about your sector. Ends with a diagnosis of the market gap specific to your situation.

**Phase 2 — Opportunity Map**
Finds real grants, contracts, and pre-RFP signals in your sector. Scores each one: MOVE NOW (85+), MOVE THIS MONTH (70-84), WATCH (50-69). Names your angle and who to partner with.

**Phase 3 — Playbook Foundation**
7 questions that capture your execution approach across scan, analyze, position, partner, deploy, fund, and scale.

**Phase 4 — Digital Employee Match**
Recommends the one DE role matched to your highest-leverage gap. Ends with a Find Your Start prompt.
