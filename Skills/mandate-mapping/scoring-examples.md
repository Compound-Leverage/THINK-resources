# Scoring Examples - Sample Opportunities with Rationale

> **Rubric:** All examples are scored on the CL-wide **Capital Event (CE) rubric** — the
> same bands implemented in `score_event.py` (Signal Finder Toolkit) and defined in the
> capital-event-radar skill. Funding: $1M+=40, $250K-$1M=30, $50-249K=20, <$50K=10.
> Timeline: Pre-RFP/planning=20, 6-12mo=15, 3-6mo=10, <3mo=5. Competition:
> Unsolicited/sole-source=20, Limited=15, Set-aside=10, Open=5. Geography: Target
> (DE/MD/PA/VA/DC)=20, Federal w/ local delivery=15, Adjacent=10, Out-of-region=0.
> The CE rubric also allows bonuses (+10 unsolicited/multi-year/planning grant, +5
> partnership/regional/intermediary-eligible) and a −5 penalty (single-state non-target /
> previous-awardee preference). The examples below show four-factor base scores (no
> bonuses applied) so they reproduce exactly from `score_event.py` defaults.

## Scoring Patterns and Insights

CE tiers: **🔴 Synthesize Now (85-100)** · **🟡 Synthesize This Week (70-84)** · **🟢 Nurture (50-69)** · **⚪ Monitor (<50)**.

### 🔴 Synthesize Now (85-100) Indicators
- $1M+ funding
- Pre-RFP / planning / unsolicited stage (timeline 20 + competition 20)
- Target state geography
- Examples: MD-WF-2025-001, PA-AI-2025-008, MD-AI-2025-007

### 🟡 Synthesize This Week (70-84) Characteristics
- Strong funding with an early positioning window
- One factor held below full value (federal geography, open competition)
- Often crosses into 🔴 once a partnership or unsolicited bonus applies
- Example: FED-WF-2025-021 (80)

### 🟢 Nurture (50-69) Characteristics
- Mid funding band ($50-249K) OR open competition OR adjacent geography
- Active-RFP timelines (<3mo / 3-6mo) instead of a pre-RFP window
- Relationship building; pursue selectively with a partner or bonus to lift the score
- Examples: VA-ED-2025-012 (65), DE-WF-2025-003 (55), NC-WF-2025-014 (55), DC-ED-2025-006 (50), VA-WF-2025-018 (50)

### ⚪ Monitor (<50) Indicators
- Sub-$50K funding and/or multiple barriers (timeline, fit, competition)
- Not a strategic priority — archive, don't pursue
- Example: PA-ED-2025-019 (40)

### Geography and Competition Discipline the Score
- Under the CE rubric, adjacent/out-of-region geography (10 / 0) and open competition (5) meaningfully cap otherwise strong opportunities.
- A $500K adjacent-state open grant (NC-WF-2025-014) lands at 55 / Nurture, not high priority.
- Pre-RFP positioning and restricted competition (unsolicited / limited / set-aside) are where the points are.

### Bonuses and Penalties
- The CE rubric layers modifiers on the four-factor base: **+10** (unsolicited / multi-year / planning grant), **+5** (partnership-required / regional / intermediary-eligible / pre-proposal conference), **−5** (single-state non-target / previous-awardee preference).
- Examples above show base scores only. Apply modifiers via `score_event.py` to see borderline opportunities (e.g., FED-WF-2025-021 at 80 → 85 with a partnership bonus) shift tier.

## Using These Examples

**For Training:**
- Use these examples to calibrate scoring consistency against the CE rubric
- Discuss as team which opportunities you would actually pursue
- Reproduce any score with `score_event.py` to confirm consistency

**For Quality Control:**
- Compare new opportunities to these examples
- If score doesn't "feel right," review scoring logic against the CE bands
- Look for patterns in missed opportunities

**For Reporting:**
- Reference similar past opportunities when briefing new ones
- "This is similar to MD-WF-2025-001 which scored 100"
- Build institutional knowledge over time
