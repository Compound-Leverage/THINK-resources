# Proposal Generator (proposal-generator-open) -- Deliverable Templates

Blank version of the document the Proposal Generator produces. Use this directly (fill in
your own data by hand) or as the reference format the plugin's output should match. Every
`[bracket]` is a placeholder.

This is a single-persona, single-workflow plugin: one skill runs all 7 phases (Intake,
Research, Assessment, Strategy, Draft, QA, Output) for one deal at a time. The template
below is the final deliverable from Phase 7 - the proposal document itself - structured
by the section list Phase 5 of the skill uses for a **Commercial** deal (private sector,
ROI-focused). Government, Partnership, and Simple/SOW deals use a different section set;
the fully pre-built version of each is already in the plugin at
[`references/templates/`](../Plugins/proposal-generator-open/references/templates/)
(`government-style.md`, `partnership-style.md`, `simple-sow.md`) - pull from there instead
of rebuilding by hand.

### Proposal Generator - Proposal Document (Commercial style)

```
[Proposal Title]
Prepared for: [Client name]
Prepared by: [Your company]
Date: [Date]

## Executive Summary
The situation you're facing: [Client's specific pain, quantified]
What we'll deliver: [Outcomes, not features]
Why this matters now: [Urgency / cost of inaction]
The investment: $[Amount] over [Timeline], expected Year 1 return of $[Amount]
The next step: [Specific action with timeline]

## The Challenge You're Facing

| What it's costing you | Amount |
|---|---|

- Outcome 3: [Name] - [Description]
How it works:
| Phase | Weeks | What happens | What you'll see |

| [Phase 1 name] | [Range] | [Description] | [Milestone] |
| [Phase 2 name] | [Range] | [Description] | [Milestone] |
| [Phase 3 name] | [Range] | [Description] | [Milestone] |

## Your Investment
| What's included | Investment |
|---|---|
| [Component 1] | $[Amount] |
| [Component 2] | $[Amount] |
| Total investment | $[Amount] |
ROI: current cost $[Amount]/yr vs. solution cost $[Amount]/yr = $[Savings]/yr saved

## Why [Your Company]

3. [Differentiator 3] - [Specific proof]
```
